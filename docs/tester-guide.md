# Closed Testing Guide & Feedback Form — WRONG WAY!

## Welcome Testers!

Thank you for testing **WRONG WAY!** (`io.github.kaustubhtote.wrongway`).

### How to Install
1. Open the Google Play closed-testing opt-in link provided by the publisher on your Android phone.
2. Accept the tester invitation and install **WRONG WAY!** from Google Play.
3. Keep the app installed and test across multiple days during the closed-testing window.

### What to Test (5-Minute Checklist)
1. **First Launch & Tutorial:** Verify the splash screen and How-To-Play tutorial make the opposite-direction rule (`LEFT → tap RIGHT`) crystal clear.
2. **Classic Mode:** Play a full round until 3 mistakes. Confirm each wrong tap or timeout deducts exactly 1 life.
3. **60s Rush Mode:** Play a full 60-second round. Confirm score cannot drop below 0 and the round ends cleanly at 0.0s.
4. **Pause & Interruptions:** Press Back or switch apps mid-round; verify the game auto-pauses and timers do not tick while backgrounded.
5. **Controls & Readability:** Check button comfort (thumb reach, no missed taps) and readability on your screen size/navigation bar (gesture or 3-button).
6. **Settings & Persistence:** Toggle Sound, Haptics, and Reduced Motion, force-close and reopen the app, and verify settings and Best Scores persist.
