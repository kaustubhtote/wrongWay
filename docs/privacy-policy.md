## Overview

**WRONG WAY!** is an offline-only arcade game. Your privacy is respected by design: we do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

## Data We Collect

**None.**
- No account registration or sign-in is required.
- No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
- The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission or access network services.

## Local On-Device Storage

The app stores non-sensitive gameplay preferences and local high scores solely on your device in the app's private storage directory (`settings.json` and `records.json`):
- Sound, haptics, reduced-motion, and tutorial-completion preferences.
- Local Classic and 60s Rush best scores, streaks, and up to 20 recent session summaries.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

## Sharing Feature

If you tap **Share** on the Result screen, the app prepares a plain-text summary of your score for your device's system share sheet or copies it to your clipboard only when you explicitly tap Share.

## Children's Privacy

Because **WRONG WAY!** collects zero personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

If a future version introduces optional online features, this Privacy Policy will be updated prior to release.
